# Events & Field Notes

I learn in the real world—where people build, ship, debate, and connect.

This space captures the events and volunteer rooms I’ve been part of, with a focus on what I _observed_, what I _learned_, and how it shaped the way I build AI products and full-stack systems.

## What you’ll find here

### Tech Events

- Product and engineering insights from keynotes, demos, and hands-on sessions
- Patterns I notice across teams: what scales, what breaks, what actually ships
- Notes on tools, workflows, and “how good products feel” in practice

### Volunteer & Community Work

- Behind-the-scenes views of high-stakes operations and coordination
- How I communicate, support teams, and stay calm in fast-moving environments
- Reflections on service, responsibility, and leadership without titles

## My lens

I’ve lived and studied across China, Japan, and the U.S.  
I’m interested in technology as culture—how systems shape behavior, and how great builders earn trust through quality.
